Hello, I'm Jarrett

Jarrett Michael is a designer, consultant, and co-founder with more than 15 years of experience across UX, product, and brand design. His career spans global design agencies, Fortune 500 companies, and 501(c)(3) nonprofits, along with service on the Graphic Design Advisory Board at Northern Virginia Community College.

Jarrett approaches design with curiosity and a systems perspective, seeking to understand the people, context, and processes that shape his work. He’s particularly interested in the gaps between organizational intent and lived experience, and in how design can help bring clarity to ambiguity.

As co-founder and Principal Creative at Bridg Consulting Firm, Jarrett brings that perspective to his work with churches and faith-based organizations, helping leaders navigate organizational change and communication challenges. His practice at Bridg reaches beyond traditional UX design to include executive strategy, organizational thinking, and the realities of building a business from the ground up.

Much of Jarrett’s broader curiosity centers on meaning and human experience. He has a particular interest in theological anthropology and its implications for how design shapes the way people understand, engage with, and participate in the world around them. His education includes degrees in Communication Design and Interactive Design from Northern Virginia Community College, along with a UX Certification from Nielsen Norman Group and certificates in Biblical Studies and Biblical History from the AWKNG School of Theology.

Outside of his professional work, Jarrett has spent more than two decades writing and performing music and is a dedicated student of ancient Near Eastern history. He lives in Virginia with his wife, Jess, and their five children.

Design philosophy

Understand first. Then make.

Good design is mostly decided before anything gets drawn. I aim to spend about 80% of a project understanding, and 20% executing. The more I know about the people, the problem, and the constraints, the fewer decisions are left to guesswork.

80% Understand20%

01

Listen

Interviews, observation, and the questions behind the request. What is actually going on, and for whom?

02

Map

Systems, constraints, and existing patterns, so the whole picture is visible before any solution is proposed.

03

Decide

A clear strategy and a short list of principles, so each design choice can be traced back to something learned.

04

Execute

Design and build quickly and with confidence, because the hard thinking is already done.
